FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is BuildQuant free?
Yes. All calculators, the project list and exports are free with no account. Your data stays in your browser.
How accurate are the estimates?
Formulas follow standard construction practice (dry volume factor, standard densities, rebar weights). Always add your own contingency for critical orders.
Can I combine calculators?
Yes — every result can be added to your project material list, which you export as one CSV or PDF.
Do I need an account?
No. Everything runs locally in your browser, with session export/import to move between devices.
